## The Brand

It's a website for RamNayan Dairy — a brand site built around one idea: **purity has two roots**. The site opens on a two-world experience. One world is rich with pure dairy tradition — ghee, dahi, paneer, lassi, buttermilk, kulfi, peda, made the slow way. The other is rooted deep in fertile earth — vermicompost, natural compost, liquid fertiliser, guavas, lemons — the soil that makes the dairy possible.

Nothing processed. Nothing rushed. The whole site moves at farm pace.

## The Story

**Our Story is a Return to Our Roots.** The founder, **S.S. Chaurasia**, left a life of engineering — precision, deadlines, success — for an open field. He saw the disconnect between people and the food they ate: processed ingredients, forgotten traditions. RamNayan is his answer, and the site tells it straight: from a corporate desk to an open field.

- **A Year on the Farm** — the farm across seasons: summer, monsoon, winter
- **Direct Booking with the Founder** — farm visits booked directly with the owner, no middlemen

---

## Experience & Motion

The site is built to feel alive, not templated:

- **Two-world transitions** — cinematic page curtain sweeps between dairy and farm
- **GSAP ScrollTrigger** reveals — content rises as you scroll
- **Lenis smooth scrolling** — buttery, weighted motion throughout
- **Preloader** — a branded entry moment before the first frame
- **Particle canvas** — ambient floating dust over the hero
- **Glassmorphism cards** — frosted panels, animated gradient borders
- **Material Symbols** iconography with rounded filled style
- **Back-to-top** and fully responsive mobile navigation
